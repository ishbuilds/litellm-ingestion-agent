# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

- Be kind, respectful, and constructive.
- Accept feedback gracefully; assume good intent.
- No harassment, trolling, insults, or unwelcome advances — public or private.

## Enforcement

Violations can be reported to the maintainer via a private channel (see
`SECURITY.md` for contact guidance). The maintainer may remove comments,
commits, or contributors who violate this code.

*Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1.*
